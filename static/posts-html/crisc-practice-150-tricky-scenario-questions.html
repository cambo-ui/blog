CRISC Scenario Tips

  1. Read carefully - Scenario questions often hide the key clue in one word (FIRST, BEST, GREATEST, PRIMARY).
  2. Answer the question asked - Don't fall for technically true statements that don't answer the actual question.
  3. Risk ownership - Business managers and senior management own risk; IT and risk functions support.
  4. FIRST steps - Usually involve understanding, analyzing, or validating before acting.
  5. GREATEST concern - Look for the option with the broadest or most strategic impact.
  6. MOST important - Usually related to business objectives, risk ownership, or governance.
  7. KRI vs KPI vs KCI - KRI = risk indicator; KPI = performance indicator; KCI = control indicator.
  8. Risk responses - Accept, Avoid, Transfer, Mitigate. Insurance = transfer; stopping activity = avoid.
  9. Control testing - Independent testing is the most reliable evidence of effectiveness.
  10. Gap analysis - Identifies the difference between current and desired states.

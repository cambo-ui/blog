1 What is the BEST definition of risk appetite?
Answer: B - The amount of risk an organization is willing to accept in pursuit of its objectives Risk appetite is the amount and type of risk an organization is willing to accept in pursuit of its strategic objectives. It is set at the organizational level by the Board and senior management.
2 What is the BEST definition of risk tolerance?
Answer: C - The acceptable level of variation in outcomes relative to risk appetite Risk tolerance is the acceptable level of variation an organization is willing to accept around specific objectives. It is more granular than risk appetite and defines the boundaries within which risk can be taken.
3 Which of the following BEST describes the relationship between risk appetite and risk tolerance?
Answer: A - Risk appetite is strategic and high-level; risk tolerance is tactical and more specific Risk appetite is a strategic, high-level statement of how much risk the organization is willing to take. Risk tolerance translates that appetite into specific, measurable boundaries for individual objectives or risk categories.
4 Who is PRIMARILY responsible for setting the organization's risk appetite?
Answer: D - The Board of Directors and senior management Risk appetite is set at the highest level of the organization - typically by the Board of Directors with input from senior management. It reflects the organization's strategic objectives and overall risk culture.
5 An organization states it has a "low risk appetite" for cybersecurity risk. What does this MOST likely mean?
Answer: B - The organization is willing to accept only minimal cybersecurity risk and will invest significantly in controls A low risk appetite means the organization is willing to accept only minimal risk in that area. It will typically invest in strong controls to reduce risk exposure to acceptable levels.
6 What is risk capacity?
Answer: C - The maximum amount of risk the organization can absorb before it cannot achieve its objectives Risk capacity is the maximum level of risk an organization can withstand before it threatens its ability to achieve objectives or its very survival. Risk appetite should always be within risk capacity.
7 Which of the following is the CORRECT relationship between risk appetite, risk tolerance, and risk capacity?
Answer: A - Risk appetite should be less than or equal to risk capacity Risk appetite must be set within risk capacity. If an organization sets its appetite above its capacity, it risks exceeding what it can absorb, which could threaten its survival. Risk tolerance operates within appetite.
8 An organization's risk appetite statement should PRIMARILY be aligned with:
Answer: D - The organization's strategic objectives and business strategy Risk appetite must be aligned with the organization's strategic objectives. It defines how much risk the organization is willing to take to achieve those objectives.
9 Which of the following is a KEY benefit of having a clearly defined risk appetite?
Answer: B - It guides decision-making and resource allocation for risk management A clear risk appetite guides decision-making, helps prioritize risk management efforts, and ensures resources are allocated where they are most needed. It does not eliminate risk or guarantee compliance.
10 A risk is assessed as being WITHIN the organization's risk tolerance. What is the MOST appropriate action?
Answer: C - Accept the risk and continue monitoring If a risk is within tolerance, it can be accepted, but monitoring should continue to ensure it remains within tolerance. Risks should only be escalated if they exceed tolerance.
11 A risk is assessed as EXCEEDING the organization's risk tolerance. What is the MOST appropriate action?
Answer: A - Escalate the risk and implement additional controls or mitigation When a risk exceeds tolerance, it must be escalated and mitigated. Accepting it would expose the organization to unacceptable risk. Changing appetite or tolerance to match the risk is not appropriate.
12 Which of the following BEST describes a risk appetite statement?
Answer: D - A formal declaration of how much risk the organization is willing to accept in pursuit of its objectives A risk appetite statement is a formal, documented declaration that articulates the amount and type of risk the organization is willing to accept. It guides risk-based decisions across the organization.
13 Risk appetite should be reviewed and updated:
Answer: B - Periodically and when significant changes occur in the business environment or strategy Risk appetite should be reviewed periodically and updated when there are significant changes in business strategy, market conditions, regulatory requirements, or the risk landscape. It is not a one-time exercise.
14 Which of the following is MOST likely to have a HIGHER risk appetite?
Answer: C - A technology startup pursuing rapid market growth A technology startup pursuing rapid growth typically has a higher risk appetite to innovate and capture market share. Organizations in safety-critical industries (hospitals, nuclear plants, central banks) typically have much lower risk appetites.
15 What does a "risk-averse" organization typically have?
Answer: A - A low risk appetite and strict risk tolerance levels A risk-averse organization has a low appetite for risk and sets strict tolerance levels. It prefers stability and predictability over higher-risk opportunities.
16 Which of the following BEST describes how risk tolerance differs from risk appetite?
Answer: D - Risk tolerance defines specific measurable boundaries within the broader risk appetite Risk tolerance provides specific, measurable boundaries for acceptable variation within the broader risk appetite. It translates the strategic appetite into operational limits.
17 An organization's risk appetite statement says it has "moderate appetite for operational risk." A business unit wants to launch a project with high operational risk. What should happen?
Answer: B - The project should be escalated and reviewed against the risk appetite before approval Proposed initiatives that may exceed the stated risk appetite should be escalated for review. The organization may decide to accept, mitigate, or reject the risk, but the decision should be made at the appropriate level.
18 Which of the following is a KEY characteristic of an effective risk appetite statement?
Answer: C - It is clearly communicated and linked to measurable tolerances An effective risk appetite statement is clearly communicated throughout the organization and linked to measurable tolerances and KRIs. It should be actionable, not vague.
19 What is the PRIMARY risk of having a risk appetite that is set ABOVE the organization's risk capacity?
Answer: A - The organization may take on more risk than it can absorb, threatening its survival Risk appetite must always be within risk capacity. Setting appetite above capacity means the organization may take on more risk than it can absorb, potentially leading to catastrophic outcomes.
20 Which of the following BEST explains why risk appetite and risk tolerance must be clearly defined?
Answer: D - To enable consistent, risk-informed decision-making across the organization Clearly defined risk appetite and tolerance enable consistent, risk-informed decision-making across the organization. They provide a framework for determining which risks are acceptable and which require action.

Quick Reference Summary
Concept Definition Set By
Risk Appetite Amount of risk willing to accept in pursuit of objectives Board / Senior Management
Risk Tolerance Acceptable variation around specific objectives Management within appetite
Risk Capacity Maximum risk the organization can absorb Determined by financial/operational strength

Exam Tips

  1. Risk Appetite = Strategic, high-level, "how much risk do we want to take?"
  2. Risk Tolerance = Tactical, specific, "how much variation can we accept?"
  3. Risk Capacity = Maximum, "how much risk can we survive?"
  4. Appetite must be within capacity. Tolerance must be within appetite.
  5. Board sets appetite. Management sets tolerance within appetite.
  6. Risks within tolerance = Accept and monitor. Risks exceeding tolerance = Escalate and mitigate.
  7. Appetite and tolerance must be reviewed periodically and when the business environment changes.

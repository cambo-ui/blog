**Step-by-Step Instructions**

- **Download and Install:** Go to the official website of your VPN provider or your device’s app store (Google Play Store, Apple App Store, or Windows/Mac installer) and install the application. [[1](https://surfshark.com/blog/how-to-use-a-vpn), [2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=29KPyLr8Ou0)]
- **Sign In:** Open the app and log in using your account credentials. [[1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=29KPyLr8Ou0), [2](https://surfshark.com/blog/how-to-use-a-vpn)]
- **Choose a Server:**
  - For **fastest speeds**, pick a server geographically close to your actual location.
  - To **access region-locked content** (such as streaming libraries from another country), select a server located in that specific country. [[1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a9updXFg8fw&t=20), [2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=29KPyLr8Ou0), [3](https://www.cnet.com/tech/services-and-software/how-to-use-a-vpn/)]
- **Connect:** Press the main **"Connect"** or **"Quick-connect"** button. Wait a second or two until the app shows a connected status (often turning green). [[1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=29KPyLr8Ou0), [2](https://surfshark.com/blog/how-to-use-a-vpn)]
- **Verify Your IP:** Search online for *"what is my public IP address"* to confirm that your IP and virtual location have changed to match the VPN server. [[1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P_ZV6tW6D34&t=9)]
- **Disconnect:** When you are finished, open the app and click **"Disconnect"** to return to your normal internet traffic.
